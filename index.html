From UFA Throw Data to Possession Patterns: A Visual Browser for How Ultimate Teams Move the Disc

Apr 2026 – Aug 2026

Developed a Python data pipeline and interactive HTML browser to turn Shown Space throw-level data from 2026 UFA games into possession-level field paths. Aggregated throws into goals and turnovers, calculated aEC per throw, completion probability, huck and reset usage, and geometry-first features such as field width, directness, side switches, and zone usage. Built filters, overlays, draggable arrangements, team navigation, and saved layouts to compare recurring offensive shapes across 22 teams.
